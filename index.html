Tax forms, turned into heroes

Hi, I’m Mum Monson, the storyteller and founder behind The Tax Files.

For six years as a U.S. tax advisor and strategist, I searched for a way to make tax literacy stick. My breakthrough came when I traded dry numbers for narrative. What began as a personal struggle sprouted into The Tax Files—an engaging approach to financial education where IRS codes, schedules, and tax forms are the seeds, and storytelling is what makes them grow.

In my world, tax forms are the main characters and true heroes. Through their journeys, my clients finally see how a Schedule C or a Retirement strategy directly impacts their marginal line. Watching my clients’ curiosity spark with each turning page has been the ultimate reward.

I believe our community begins with a story to protect our hard-earned money, and the courage to start your small business. By translating dense IRS frameworks into relatable stories, I help small business owners confidently lower their liabilities and maximize deductions.

I grew up translating for my parents and the community by helping them fill out business forms and explaining tax codes and financial statements. This is my story, and I believe our community starts out with a small business owner and later grows into an ecosystem of businesses.

And because I continuously refresh my continuing education credits, you can rest assured that your business is always growing on a rock-solid, compliant foundation. Thank you for giving me an opportunity to serve you and your organization.
